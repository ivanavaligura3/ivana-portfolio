CLIENT PROJECT

Centurion Gradnja

A modern business website created to present construction and tiling services, showcase completed work and make it easy for potential clients to get in touch.

Client Project Business Website Responsive Design
Role Frontend Development
Year 2026
Type Client Project

OVERVIEW

Building a professional digital presence for a local business.

Centurion Gradnja is a business website created for a local construction and tiling service provider.

The goal was to create a professional online presence that clearly communicates the company's services while showcasing completed projects and previous work.

The website was designed with a strong focus on visual presentation, responsive layouts and simple communication between potential clients and the business.

THE CHALLENGE

Turning practical services into a clear digital experience.

For a local service business, a website needs to communicate trust, quality and the range of services quickly.

The challenge was to organize information about services and completed projects in a way that feels professional while remaining simple to navigate.

Another important aspect was making it easy for potential clients to contact the business directly from the website.

KEY FEATURES

A business-focused website built around visibility and contact.

01

Service Presentation

Clearly presenting the company's construction and tiling services through structured content.

02

Project Showcase

A visual presentation of completed work designed to help potential clients understand the quality and scope of previous projects.

03

Responsive Layout

The website adapts to different screen sizes for a consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

04

WhatsApp Integration

A direct WhatsApp contact option makes it easier for potential clients to start a conversation with the business.

05

Clear Navigation

Information is organized into clear sections so visitors can quickly find services, company information and contact options.

06

Search-Friendly Structure

The website structure and content were organized with basic SEO considerations in mind to improve online visibility.
