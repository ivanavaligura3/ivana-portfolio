FEATURED · GRADUATION PROJECT

NutriPlan

Full-stack nutrition planning application for smarter meal organization and food management.

Web Application Graduation Project Information Systems
Role Full-stack Development
Year 2026
Type Academic Project

OVERVIEW

Turning meal planning into a structured digital experience.

NutriPlan is a full-stack web application developed as my graduation project in Information Systems.

The application was created to simplify weekly meal planning, recipe management, nutrition tracking and food inventory management within one integrated system.

Instead of relying on separate tools for planning meals, managing ingredients and creating shopping lists, NutriPlan brings these processes together in one application.

THE CHALLENGE

Solving everyday problems around meal organization.

Planning meals on a weekly basis can become difficult when recipes, ingredients, nutrition information and shopping needs are managed separately.

NutriPlan was designed around the need for a more organized approach to meal planning and food management.

KEY FEATURES

One application, multiple connected workflows.

01

Weekly Meal Planning

Organizing meals throughout the week through a structured planning interface.

02

Recipe Management

Creating, editing and managing recipes together with their ingredients and nutritional information.

03

Nutrition Tracking

Keeping track of nutritional values connected to meals and recipes.

04

Food Inventory

Managing available ingredients and keeping an overview of food supplies.

05

Shopping List

Automatically generating a shopping list based on planned meals and required ingredients.

06

User Authentication

Providing user registration and login functionality for personalized data management.
